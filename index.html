About Me

Mastery Is Not Just a Skill, It Is a Calling

My name is Liudmyla Shyshyk — a certified dental technician from Kharkiv. For over 15 years I have specialised in fabricating dental prostheses, crowns, bridges, and orthodontic appliances. Every piece I create is a synthesis of precision, aesthetics, and functionality.

I completed my training at the Medical Academy in Lutsk and undertook internships at clinics in Ukraine and Germany. I continuously refine my skills, embracing the latest materials and technologies — including digital workflows and zirconia dioxide milling.

"Every smile I help restore is my greatest reward."

Workflow

From Concept to Perfect Result

Impression Taking

We receive accurate impressions or digital scans (via intraoral scanner) with detailed documentation of the clinical situation and the dentist's requirements.

1
2

Diagnosis & Planning

Thorough analysis of occlusion, bite, and aesthetic parameters. Selection of optimal materials and technologies for each specific case.

Digital Design

Designing the restoration in a 3D environment (exocad / 3Shape). Design approval with the dentist prior to production. Wax-up for complex cases.

3
4

Fabrication & Finishing

5-axis milling, crystallisation, application of individual ceramics. Multi-layer characterisation for a natural appearance.

Quality Control

Verification on models, microscopic marginal fit inspection, shade and occlusion assessment. Only a perfect result is delivered to the dentist.
